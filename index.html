A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before the first cut.

What does an edit cost?+

Every project is different. Send your footage length, target video length, style references, and deadline. We’ll discuss the scope and provide a project quote before work begins.

Can we work together regularly?+

Yes. Whether you’re building a weekly YouTube schedule or a series of social videos, tell us your publishing cadence and we can discuss an ongoing editing arrangement.

What should I send to get started?+

A short description of your project, the platform, your deadline, and a reference or two. If your footage is ready, include a shareable download link in your email.

How do timelines and revisions work?+

We agree on a delivery timeline and revision scope with your quote. These depend on the footage, complexity, and number of deliverables, so you know what to expect before the first cut.
